Welcome to LarpedIn

Solves LinkedIn's daily puzzles right on the page: it reads the board, finds the answer in milliseconds, and plays it for you.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to LinkedIn

How to use it

1

Open a puzzle

Go to any game below on linkedin.com. A small card appears in the corner of the page.

2

Press Solve

Click Solve on the card or press Alt S. Turn on auto-solve to skip even that.

3

Watch it play

The card shows how long reading, solving and playing took. Drag it anywhere, or fold it into a pill.

The games

Settings Saved

Keyboard shortcutWorks on any LinkedIn game page.
AppearanceLight is the default. Match device follows your system setting.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no servers, no analytics. Puzzles you open are kept in this browser so you can export them from the card's settings.

If something looks off

Reload the LinkedIn tab once after installing. If a solve fails, the card shows why and offers Copy debug info for a bug report.
